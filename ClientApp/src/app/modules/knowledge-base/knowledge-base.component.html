Architecture & Workflow

Master Hierarchy

2. Master Academic Setup Sequence

Strict numerical dependency order for establishing a new school or campus without relational errors.

Dependency Rule

Classes must be created before Sections. Fee Heads must be created before Fee Templates. Both Classes and Fee Templates must exist before enrolling students or generating bills.

Seq Master Entity Web Route Operational Purpose
01 Tenant Setup /tenant Define top-level organization/licensee. Associated with root permissions.
02 Institute Profile /school/institute School Name, EIIN, Address, Logo, and primary contact details.
03 Branch / Campus /school/branch Physical campuses operating under the institute (e.g. Main Campus).
04 Financial Year /school/financial-year Define fiscal accounting period (e.g., FY 2026-2027) with date boundaries.
05 Academic Session /school/academic-session Active school year (e.g. Session 2026). Drives admissions and promotions.
06 Academic Classes /school/academic-class Configure classes offered (Play, Nursery, Class 1 through Class 10).
07 Class Sections /school/section Add classroom divisions (Section A, Section B) per Class.
08 School Shifts /school/shift Define operational timings (Morning Shift, Day Shift).
09 Student Groups /school/student-group Academic streams: General for junior grades; Science, Arts, Commerce for high school.
10 Subjects & Codes /school/subject Define curriculum courses (Bangla, English, Math) and link to Classes.
11 Grading Scale /school/grading-scale GPA grade boundaries (A+: 80-100%, GPA 5.0 down to F: <33%, GPA 0.0).
12 Fee Heads /school/fee-head Register revenue categories: Tuition Fee, Admission Fee, Exam Fee, Lab, Transport.
13 Fee Templates /school/fee-template Bundle Fee Heads with fixed pricing packages mapped to Class, Shift, and Group.
Student Lifecycle

3. Student Onboarding & Registry Flow

Choose between interactive single admission or bulk onboarding via Excel spreadsheet.

Pathway A: Single Admission

Route: /school/admission

  • Select Branch, Session, Class, Section, Shift, Group.
  • Enter Student Full Name, Gender, DOB, Blood Group.
  • Enter Father, Mother, Guardian Phone & Address.
  • Upload student photograph (.jpg, .png).
  • Click Save ➔ Auto-generates unique Student ID & Roll.

Pathway B: Excel Bulk Import

Route: /school/student-list

  • Click 'Download Sample Excel' (or use TestStudentUpload.xlsx).
  • Populate columns: Name, Roll, Class, Section, Shift, Phone.
  • Click 'Upload Student Excel' button.
  • System validates all rows in a single database transaction.
  • Batch imports 100+ students instantly.
Excel Import Rules

Do not alter header column names. Ensure Class Name, Section Name, and Shift Name match database entities exactly (case-sensitive). Guardian phone numbers must be 11-digit mobile numbers for SMS delivery.

Fee Management

4. Billing Lifecycle, Bill Updates & Collections

Complete invoicing cycle from batch generation to payment collection and receipt printing.

STEP 1

Batch Monthly Bill Processing

/school/bill-process

Admin selects Academic Session, Target Month (e.g. November), and Class. The system pulls amounts from the Fee Template and generates unpaid invoices for all active students.

STEP 2

Decision: Bill Updates or Special Waivers?

/school/bill-collection (Update Bill)

If a student has a scholarship, fee waiver, or penalty, open 'Update Bill' to modify specific fee heads. Otherwise, invoices proceed directly to collection.

Counter Collection

/school/bill-collection
Search student by ID/Roll. View outstanding dues. Collect cash and click 'Pay Now'.

Multi-Month Advance

/school/multi-month-bill-collection
Collect fees for 3, 6, or 12 consecutive months in a single advance receipt.

Online bKash / PayBill

Parents pay remotely via bKash or automated bank bill-pay. The system verifies TxnID and marks bill Paid in real time.

STEP 4

Instant 2-Copy Money Receipt & Ledger Post

/school/student-bill-history

Generates an instant 2-copy printed Money Receipt (Office Copy & Student Copy). Cash automatically debits the Cashbook, clearing student dues and unlocking exam Admit Cards.

Financial Ledgers

5. Financial Accounting & Audit Statements

Automated double-entry bookkeeping feeding real-time Trial Balance and Balance Sheet.

Report Name Web Route Financial Functionality
Daily Cashbook /school/cashbook Tracks daily counter cash collections, petty cash expenses, cash in hand, and opening/closing balances.
Bankbook /school/bankbook Reconciles digital bKash payments, bank deposits, cheques, bank fees, and institutional bank balances.
Trial Balance /school/trial-balance Real-time equilibrium across all accounts. Mathematically guarantees Total Debits = Total Credits.
Income Statement /school/income-statement Profit & Loss statement: Total tuition revenue minus staff salaries and operational expenses over any date range.
Balance Sheet /school/balance-sheet Institution assets (cash, bank, campus equipment) vs liabilities and accumulated capital as of cutoff date.
Academic Assessments

6. Examinations, Marks Entry & Fee-Gated Admit Cards

Multi-component mark entry, GPA calculations, and fee-due verification gatekeeper.

STAGE 1

Exam Components & Weights

Define Exam Types (Class Test, Half Yearly, Final Exam) and score distribution: Creative / Theory (CQ), Multiple Choice (MCQ), Practical, Assignment.

STAGE 2

Batch Marks Entry & GPA Calculation

/school/student-mark-entry

Teacher enters student component scores. System dynamically calculates Total Marks, Letter Grade (A+, A, B, F), and Grade Point (5.0 to 0.0) automatically.

Gatekeeper Check: Has Student Cleared Tuition Fees?

Before issuing or downloading an Admit Card, the system checks whether the student has any outstanding unpaid monthly dues up to the exam cutoff date.

Fee Paid: Issue Admit Card

Vector-sharp PDF generates instantly with student photo, barcode, exam routine, and signature lines. Ready to print.

Unpaid Dues: Blocked

Download blocked with alert. Student must settle dues at the counter. (Admin override: check IgnoreDueCheck).
